# English corpus manifest

Bibliographic record for [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Research and independent review: 7 November 2026. Full article texts are not stored in this repository. Follow the URL to the publisher's page. Nothing here is a native-speaker biography, and no row certifies that the current page is byte-for-byte pre-2020 wording.

## Acceptance used for this skill

The installed skill uses the publication-date criterion: original publication strictly before 2020-11-07 (more than six years before 7 November 2026). It does not claim an archived snapshot of every current sentence.

- 30/30 distinct articles. 15 authors. First-publication dates below all pass that cutoff. The earliest is 2002-11-11 and the latest is 2020-04-06.
- 30/30 were read in full, twice, from the current original textual bodies (researcher, then independent reviewer). Notes carried with those bodies were included.
- 30/30 have contextual evidence that the article was originally authored in English, not translated from another source text. That evidence is bylines, own examples, and outward translation links. It is not a finding about first language, ethnicity, or nationality.
- 1/30 (EN25) also had a complete pre-cutoff archived body read. Its current page is not certified identical to that archive.
- 29/30 have no complete pre-cutoff wording comparison. A missing update field is not proof the prose is unchanged.
- EN17 and EN24 stay in this list because they were read and their dates pass. They are **not** rule support. See [review-evidence.md](review-evidence.md).

Images, animations, embedded demos, audio, video, and linked repositories were not executed. Do not treat a source command or security step as current Coppice guidance.
